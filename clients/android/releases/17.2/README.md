# NewsBlur 17.2 for Android

Version code 305.

Premium Archive now includes 1 hour of Listen a month, and the new Premium Archive Extra includes 10 hours a month. Switch between them with the 1 hour / 10 hours toggle on the Premium Archive plan. Free and Premium accounts can now hear the first 60 seconds of any story read aloud. When more than 20 new stories arrive in a feed, tapping the new stories button now shows the first batch sooner.
