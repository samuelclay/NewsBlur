# NewsBlur Android 16.1 (299)

Meet Listen: hear stories read aloud in 21 languages. Choose voices, follow highlighted paragraphs, tap to jump, and adjust speed. Queue stories in Up Next, synced across devices, and keep listening with your screen locked. Auto-continue plays the next unread story. Full listening is included with Premium Archive and Premium Pro; Free and Premium accounts can try 30-second previews.
