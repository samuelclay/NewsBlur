# NewsBlur 17.0

Version 17.0, build 357 for iOS and macOS; Android version code 303.

## iOS

NewsBlur 17 brings a more lively reading experience. Feeds, folder headings, story titles, and article sections animate into place in the order you see them. Switching between All, Unread, Focus, and Saved is fast and fluid.

Prefer less movement? The new Reduce motion toggle in Preferences > Appearance removes sliding and scaling while keeping the gentle fades. It's off by default.

This release also smooths story loading in Gray mode, gives discovery tags more room on iPhone, and improves dialogs and controls on iPhone and iPad.

## macOS
