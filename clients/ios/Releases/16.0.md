# NewsBlur Version 16.0 Release Notes

## Version 16.0 (build 343)

### iOS What's New

NEWSBLUR IN YOUR LANGUAGE
NewsBlur now speaks 21 languages. Menus, buttons, settings, dialogs and the first-time setup appear in your language, picked up automatically from your iPhone or iPad. Your stories stay exactly as they were published: NewsBlur translates its own interface, not the feeds you read.

CHOOSE YOUR LANGUAGE ANYTIME
The first time you open the app, NewsBlur shows which language it picked next to English, so you can keep it or switch back in one tap. Change it anytime under Preferences > Language and the app switches right away, no restart needed. Your choice is saved to your account, so the web follows along.

RIGHT TO LEFT
Arabic and Hebrew switch the whole interface to read right to left.

ALSO IN THIS RELEASE
With a keyboard, story shortcuts now work while the story list has focus, and N opens the first unread story.

If a phrase reads awkwardly in your language, tell me on the NewsBlur forum and I'll fix it.

### App Review notes

Version 16.0 translates NewsBlur's interface into 21 languages, chosen automatically from the device language and changeable under Preferences > Language. On first launch in a non-English language, a one-time dialog shows the detected language beside English. Story content is not translated. Please use the existing review account credentials.
