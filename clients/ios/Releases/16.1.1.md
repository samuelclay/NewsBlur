# NewsBlur 16.1.1

iOS and macOS build 353. Source: `03430f0185a0d41288ab4513bec43edd95617748`. Tags: `iOS_16.1.1`, `macOS_16.1.1`.

## What's new

Listen is easier to control. Start listening from the reading toolbar and move on with the mini player's Next story button.

Auto-continue now follows your chosen sort order in Saved Stories and Read Stories, including oldest first. Playback continues down the list without jumping back to earlier stories.

Listen reads a headline only once when it is repeated at the start of an article.

Listening allowances are now weekly: 1 hour with Premium Archive and 10 hours with Premium Pro, resetting every Sunday. Settings and Now Playing show your remaining time in hours and minutes.
