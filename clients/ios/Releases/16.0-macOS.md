# NewsBlur for Mac 16.0

Version 16.0, build 344. This Mac Catalyst release uses the shared iOS codebase. The existing iOS 16.0 build 343 and its immutable tag are unchanged.

## What’s New

NewsBlur for Mac now speaks 21 languages. Menus, buttons, settings, and setup follow your Mac’s language, or you can choose a language from the NewsBlur menu. Your stories stay exactly as published.

Open Choose Language from the Language submenu to preview the title, prompt, and button in your selected language, with English underneath. Choose Automatic to follow your Mac’s language settings again. Arabic and Hebrew use a right-to-left interface.

Keyboard shortcuts now work while the story list has focus, and N opens the first unread story.

If a translation sounds awkward, tell us the language and where you saw it on the NewsBlur forum.

## Source and validation

The keyboard fixes from PR #2169 are included in the shared source. Native Mac verification on signed build 344 covered Command-comma, chooser Cancel, live French Preferences, Arabic/Hebrew right-to-left layout, restoring Automatic, and N opening and advancing unread stories. Fresh before/after screenshots are attached to PR #2179; test stories were restored to unread.

Store listings and release notes are in clients/store/metadata and clients/store/releases/16.0 under the macos key. Apple name/subtitle and subscription products are shared with iOS. Both translation batches were independently reviewed, including the thirteen new interface strings. The localized Preferences screen retains the existing Mac column layout, dashboard layout, and video playback choices and their saved values.
