# NewsBlur Version 15.1 Release Notes

## Version 15.1 (build 342)

### iOS What's New

SIGN IN WITH APPLE OR GOOGLE
You can now sign in with Apple or Google, or connect either one to your existing NewsBlur account. Your feeds and reading history stay with you, even if your Apple or Google email is different from your NewsBlur email. Username and password sign-in is still available.

A NEW WAY TO GET STARTED
Pick your interests and explore collections of feeds to find your first favorite sites. Preview stories before subscribing, or import your existing subscriptions from an OPML file.

SMOOTHER READING
This release improves iPhone rotation, iPad column layouts, and navigation between stories. It also includes fixes for image interactions and keeping your place while reading.
