# NewsBlur macOS 16.1

Version 16.1, build 352, submitted October 29, 2026 at 12:35 PM PDT and verified Waiting for Review. Automatic release after approval is enabled. This update is not yet publicly available.

## Changes

- Fix story titles remaining in a loading state when a notification reopens a window closed with Command-W. Notification navigation waits for the new scene's reader controllers before loading the story and title list.
- Bring Listen to Mac, including voices in 21 languages, highlighted paragraphs, playback controls, and an Up Next queue that syncs across devices. Full listening requires Premium Archive or Premium Pro; Free and Premium accounts have 30-second previews.
- Include the story-grid scrolling fix already on main.

Canonical English and translated release notes are in `clients/store/releases/16.1/` under `macos.whats_new`.
