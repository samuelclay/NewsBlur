# NewsBlur 16.1 (351)

Meet Listen, a new way to enjoy your stories.

Tap Listen to hear an article read aloud in one of 21 languages. Follow along with highlighted paragraphs, tap a paragraph to jump to it, and choose a voice for each language. Adjust delivery and playback speed from 0.5× to 3×.

Add stories to Up Next, reorder your queue, and keep listening with your screen locked or while using another app. Your queue syncs across devices. Auto-continue moves to the next unread story, and finished stories are marked read.

Listen is included with Premium Archive and Premium Pro. Free and Premium accounts can try 30-second story previews.

This update also includes smoother navigation and fixes for scrolling through the story grid.
