## Changes since Mac 16.0 build 348

- Space and Shift-Space page through articles and move to the next unread or previous story at the boundaries.
- Preferences and Keyboard Shortcuts share a configurable scroll amount.
- The story settings menu can open Keyboard Shortcuts directly.
- The Mac window restores its size and position across relaunches.
- Ask AI model labels reflect the current providers.
- Story titles update their read state immediately on selection by click, keyboard, or Next/Previous. Manual and delayed mark-read preferences are preserved.
