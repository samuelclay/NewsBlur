# NewsBlur Version 15.0.0 Release Notes

## Version 15.0.0 (build 338)

### iOS What's New

NewsBlur 15 makes finding your next favorite site easier and everyday reading smoother.

DISCOVER MORE TO READ
The redesigned Add & Discover Sites lets you swipe between search, popular sites, YouTube, Reddit, newsletters, podcasts, Google News, and web feeds. Preview stories before subscribing, see how recently a site has published, and add it straight to the folder you choose. Related Sites now has the same rich previews and Try and Add controls.

A SMOOTHER READER
Faster story lists, smoother scrolling, and more reliable reading positions help you pick up where you left off. New floating toolbars keep reading controls close at hand, with a preference to place the story-list toolbar at the top or bottom. Redesigned menus make common actions easier to find.

TAKE A CLOSER LOOK
Tap an article image to open the new full-screen viewer. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, then save, copy, or share the image.

BETTER ON IPAD
Try new feeds and open story previews while keeping your three-column reader. Discovery remembers your place when you return. Layout, rotation, and popover improvements make browsing more comfortable.

LESS SPACE, MORE RELIABILITY
Offline storage now keeps recent stories within your chosen limit and reclaims unused space automatically. Deleting offline stories clears cached article content and images and compacts storage more thoroughly. This release also includes fixes for story selection, unread counts, themes, and offline reading.
