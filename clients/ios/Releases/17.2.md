# NewsBlur 17.2 for iPhone, iPad, and Mac

Build 360.

Premium Archive now includes 1 hour of Listen a month, and the new Premium Archive Extra includes 10 hours a month. Switch between them with the 1 hour / 10 hours toggle on the Premium Archive plan. Free and Premium accounts can now hear the first 60 seconds of any story read aloud. NewsBlur also reconnects more reliably when you come back online, including after you sign in to a Wi-Fi network.

The Mac notes add one more line, for a change iPhone and iPad already got in 17.1.1: When more than 20 new stories arrive in a feed, clicking the new stories button now shows the first batch sooner.
