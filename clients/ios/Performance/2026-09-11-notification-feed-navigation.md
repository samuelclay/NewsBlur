## Reproduction

The shared iOS/Catalyst `processNotification:action:withCompletionHandler:` path selected the `notifications` river whenever any active feed had notifications enabled. The source feed and exact story hash were available in the payload but only used by the single-feed fallback when notification subscriptions were absent.

The initial simulator regression executes the production entry point for default taps and the explicit View Story action. Five tests produce nine expected assertion failures: no specific feed is selected, and the destination is `notifications`. Daily Briefing and background Read/Save/Dismiss actions already pass. Evidence: `/tmp/newsblur-scroll-evidence/notification-route-before-77.xcresult`, `/tmp/newsblur-notification-before-77.log`. The initial screenshot `/tmp/newsblur-scroll-evidence/notification-start-77.png` records the signed-in simulator setup, not the wrong destination.

After correcting routing, eight expanded tests produce eight expected assertion failures in `/tmp/newsblur-scroll-evidence/notification-lookup-before-78.xcresult`. An unread-only feed excludes already-read notification stories. Repeated startup attempts before subscriptions arrive erase the pending feed and story. A feed-list refresh re-enters the parent-folder navigation path while the selected feed lookup is in progress.

## Behavior

- Ordinary story notifications open their exact feed and search for their story hash, on iOS and Mac Catalyst.
- A single exact-hash request runs alongside the first feed page. A matching older story is included in chronological order, independently of the feed page number. Responses from an earlier account, navigation, or target are ignored.
- Later pages preserve the selected cell's position and the existing article/WebView. The extra target is deduplicated when normal pagination reaches it. Its presence does not trigger automatic loading through the entire intervening history.
- The notification visit includes read stories without changing the stored feed preference. Its later pages retain the same filter after the story is selected. Normal feed navigation or an explicit All/Unread choice ends the override.
- A pending notification survives repeated attempts while its feed is unavailable. A newer notification replaces that target; leaving the pending navigation or changing accounts clears it.
- Feed-list refresh leaves an active notification lookup on its current feed page.
- Daily Briefing and notification Read/Save/Dismiss actions retain their destinations and behavior.
