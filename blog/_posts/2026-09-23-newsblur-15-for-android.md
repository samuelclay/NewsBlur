The part of a news reader you use most is the part that's easiest to take for granted. Scroll through a folder, open a story, read a little, go back, open the next one. A small delay or a lost reading position interrupts that rhythm every time. For NewsBlur 15 on Android, I spent a lot of time on that loop.

This release brings faster startup, smoother feed and story lists, floating reading controls, and more control over gestures. It also brings the full Add + Discover Sites experience to Android, so finding something new to read is as comfortable as reading the sites you already follow.

### A smoother path through your stories

Feed and story scrolling has been reworked, along with the transitions into and out of the reader. Returning from an article highlights the story you just left, making it easier to keep your place in a long list. Folder navigation and nested folder pickers have had attention too.

The floating controls put the things you reach for near your thumb. In the story list, search, display options, and settings sit together in the toolbar. In the reader, the controls sit in smaller groups that leave more of the article visible and move out of the way as you scroll. You can choose whether the story list toolbar lives at the top or bottom in Preferences.

{% include mobile-release-phone.html image="/assets/android-15-story-list.png" alt="Android story list with thumbnails and the floating toolbar at the bottom" caption="Search and display controls stay close to the stories." %}

Gestures are configurable, with separate choices for feeds and stories. Set the swipe, tap, and long press actions to suit how you read. If you prefer to see your options, long press a story to open its action menu. The menus have clearer groups and open from the control you tapped, and appearance controls let you adjust the view without digging through a separate settings screen.

### Take a closer look

Article images now have their own viewer. Tap an image, pinch or double tap to zoom, then swipe to dismiss it and return to your article. The status and navigation bars stay in place when the viewer opens, so the page behind it doesn't jump to a different height.

{% include mobile-release-phone.html image="/assets/android-15-image-viewer.png" alt="An article image open in the Android image viewer with the phone status and navigation bars visible" caption="Open an image without losing your place in the article." %}

### Find your next favorite site

The plus button opens a compact Add Site panel. If you already have an address, paste it and choose a folder. If you're looking for something new, the shortcuts take you into discovery, including Popular and Trending sites.

{% include mobile-release-phone.html image="/assets/android-15-add-site.png" alt="Android Add Site panel with an address field, folder picker, and discovery shortcuts" caption="Paste an address or start exploring from the same panel." %}

Add + Discover Sites brings together site search, popular feeds, YouTube, Reddit, newsletters, podcasts, Google News, and Web Feeds. Search for a YouTube channel, find a subreddit, or browse for a podcast without having to track down its RSS address yourself. Web Feeds can help you follow a page that doesn't publish an RSS feed.

Results show recent story titles, images, and excerpts, along with publishing activity. Tap a story preview to read that exact article, or tap **Try** to look through the feed before subscribing. When you return, the loaded previews are still there. If a feed needs fetching, NewsBlur shows progress and gives you a way to retry.

{% include mobile-release-phone.html image="/assets/android-15-discover.png" alt="Android discovery results showing recent stories, images, and subscription controls" caption="Read a little before deciding what belongs in your subscriptions." %}

The folder picker sits beside **Add**, so a new site goes straight where you want it. Discovery remembers your folder and your List or Grid view. Related Sites uses the same rich previews and controls, so you can explore outward from a site you already like in the same way.

### The smaller things add up

There are also improvements to related stories, unread state syncing, offline reading, and opening older stories directly. Nested folders keep their identities even when names overlap. These are less visible than a new toolbar, but they matter when you're moving through a lot of reading on your phone.

NewsBlur 15 for Android is available now on the [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.newsblur). The [iOS release](/2026/09/23/newsblur-15-for-ios/) has its own reader and discovery overhaul too.

If a gesture doesn't feel right or you have an idea for making reading more comfortable, let me know on the [NewsBlur forum](https://forum.newsblur.com).
