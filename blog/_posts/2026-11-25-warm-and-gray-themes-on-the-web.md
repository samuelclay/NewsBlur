Earlier this week I changed the web's dark theme. Read stories got easier to tell apart from unread ones, and the background went from dark gray to a dark blue with brighter text. A reader on the forum [told me](https://forum.newsblur.com/t/turn-down-the-brightness-on-the-new-dark-mode-for-web/13976) the extra contrast was hard on their eyes, and asked if the old gray could come back as a theme of its own. The apps already have a gray theme, and iOS has had a warm one for years, so now the web has both. And since the only place to pick a theme was one row in a menu, I rebuilt Preferences while I was in there.

### Four themes

The web reader now comes in **Light**, **Warm**, **Gray**, and **Dark**.

<img src="/assets/web-themes-light-warm-gray-dark.webp" alt="The NewsBlur web reader shown four times with the Cooking folder and the same story open: Light and Warm on top, Gray and Dark below" style="width: 100%;border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1);margin: 24px auto;display: block;">

**Gray** is the dark theme the way it used to look, in neutral grays with softer text. It isn't an exact copy. The grays are a little lighter than they were, and it keeps this week's fixes, so read stories are still easy to tell from unread ones.

**Warm** is the cream and tan theme from the iPhone, iPad, and Mac apps, built from the same colors.

### Picking one

The theme row in the manage menu (the gear in the bottom left) and in the Manage box on the dashboard now starts with **Auto**, followed by a circle for each theme, the way the iOS apps show it. Point at a circle to see its name.

<img src="/assets/web-theme-picker.png" alt="The bottom of the manage menu in the Warm theme. Under the font, size, and spacing rows, the theme row has Auto followed by four circles for Light, Warm, Gray, and Dark, with Warm selected" style="width: 50%;border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1);margin: 24px auto;display: block;">

### Auto, with your two themes

**Auto** follows your device. It used to mean Light or Dark. Now you choose which light theme and which dark theme it switches between. Pick Gray once and Auto remembers it, so you can read in Light while your device is light and in Gray once it goes dark.

You can also set both directly. Open **Preferences** and the first thing under Appearance is every theme as a small picture of the reader, with Auto's light and dark themes right below.

### Preferences you can see

Preferences used to be four long lists of radio buttons, each one explained in a sentence. Now most settings are a row of cards, and each card is a small drawing of what that choice does to the reader. Image preview shows where the thumbnail lands. Default layout shows Full, Split, List, Grid, and Magazine as they look.

<img src="/assets/web-preferences-story-list.png" alt="The Story list group in Preferences, with a drawn card for every choice of default layout, story order, stories shown, content preview, and image preview" style="width: 90%;border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1);margin: 24px auto;display: block;">

A few other things changed along the way:

- **Five tabs.** General, Feeds, Stories, Behavior, and Keyboard. Everything about marking stories as read and what your clicks do moved into Behavior, and each tab is split into groups like Sidebar and Story list.
- **Hover for the details.** Point at any card and a line of text says exactly what it does.
- **No Save button.** A change is saved as soon as you make it, and a small Saved pill shows up next to the tabs.
- **More room.** The dialog is half again as wide and as tall as it was.
- **One Motion setting.** Animations and Reduce motion are now a single choice: Full, Reduced, or None.

<img src="/assets/web-preferences-tooltip.png" alt="The Feeds tab of Preferences with the pointer over the Most used first card under Site order, a tooltip above it reading Most used at top, then alphabetical, and a green Saved pill to the left of the tabs" style="width: 100%;border: 1px solid rgba(0,0,0,0.1);margin: 24px auto;display: block;">

Every setting that was there before is still there, and each one still does what it did.

### Availability

Warm, Gray, and the new Preferences are live on the web now. If a color in Warm or Gray looks off, or a drawing doesn't match what its setting does, let me know on the <a href="https://forum.newsblur.com">NewsBlur forum</a>.
