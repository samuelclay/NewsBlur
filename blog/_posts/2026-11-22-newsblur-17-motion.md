NewsBlur 17 is out for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Android, and the same work is live on the web. Feeds, folders, and stories now move into place as they load, in the order you read them.

### One row after the next

Open a folder and its stories come in from the top, one after the next. Open a story and its site, title, byline, tags, and text arrive in order, followed by the story buttons. The list you're leaving fades out as it looked, and menus, dialogs, and theme changes get the same treatment.

It stays out of your way. Each row fades in quickly, scrolling finishes anything still animating, and things already on screen don't move again.

### Reduce motion

If you'd rather have less of it, turn on **Reduce motion** under Appearance in Preferences, on the web, iOS, Mac, and Android. Nothing slides, but things still fade in. NewsBlur also follows your device's own Reduce Motion setting.

### A new frame for the Mac

The Mac app now has one translucent sidebar and toolbar around a rounded reading area, and new windows open with all three panes.

<img src="/assets/mac-17-reader-frame.webp" alt="The NewsBlur Mac app with a translucent sidebar and toolbar around the story list and the reader, showing the Cooking folder" style="width: 100%;margin: 24px auto;display: block;">

### A few fixes

- On iPhone, marking stories older than a number of days now updates the list on screen.
- On iPad, the Ask AI and sharing composers stay above the keyboard in landscape.
- On iPhone and Android phones, the Grid and List switch in Discover sits next to search, so the category tags get the whole row.
- On iOS, stories load more smoothly in the Gray theme.
- On Android, read titles are lighter so they're easier to tell apart from unread ones, and article headlines wrap in balanced lines.
- On Android, stories that arrive after you mark a folder as read no longer show up already read.
- On Android tablets, settings dialogs keep what you'd chosen when you rotate the screen.

### Availability

NewsBlur 17 is available now on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/newsblur/id463981119) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.newsblur). If something moves in a way that bothers you, let me know on the <a href="https://forum.newsblur.com">NewsBlur forum</a>.
